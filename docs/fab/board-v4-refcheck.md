# v4 circuit check against maker references — 2026-09-25

**Snapshot before the routed revision.** Findings below describe the schematic and board inspected at the time, not today's checked-in files. The routed revision fixes the D1 land, C11 value, GPIO pull-downs and Q2–Q4 selection and reports zero open connections (`board-v4-design.md` §§2.1, 9). TS/NTC and antenna range still need Rolf's decisions; D1 now sits downstream of the charger's input, with ESD protection there unverified. This snapshot is not permission to order.

The circuit does **not** yet match the makers' reference designs closely enough to order. In particular, the ADS1292's VCAP2 and unused GPIO wiring, the charger's missing battery-temperature sensor, an unverified FET selection and the TVS footprint need correction or an explicit safety decision before this one-board build. The checked-in v4 PCB also has **10 open connections**, including five on GND (design note §9.4); closing the schematic findings alone would not make it order-ready. This is a **schematic** review, not a routed-copper or medical-safety approval.

## Order blockers first

1. **D1 package mismatch:** schematic/BOM call for Nexperia PESD5V0L1UL but assign `Diode_SMD:D_SOD-523`; Nexperia specifies **DFN1006-2 (SOD882)**. Correct both symbol-to-footprint mapping and PCB, or select a TVS actually made in SOD-523; check orientation. [N §5]
2. **U2 VCAP2:** 100 nF C11 rather than TI's **1 µF**. Replace with an effective 1 µF capacitor close to pin 27. [A Fig. 73]
3. **U2 GPIO1/2:** pins 26/25 are NC flags in the schematic. TI says they default to inputs and **must not float**; tie each through a resistor to DGND if unused. [A §8.5.1.7]
4. **U3 TS:** fixed R13 10 kΩ to ground explicitly **disables temperature monitoring**. A 40 mAh skin-worn Li-ion cell cannot be assumed safe to charge without a cell/pack thermistor or an independently verified temperature-controlled charge arrangement. Obtain the cell's approved NTC specification and wire/check it; do not treat the resistor as a thermistor. [B §§8.3.8, 9.2.2.1.3]
5. **Q2–Q4 are not specified parts:** symbol is `2N7002`, value `NMOS`, footprint SOT-883, and the schematic has no manufacturer part number. Threshold, pin order and voltage ratings cannot be checked; name and check a real DFN1006-3 part, including turn-on at the actual gate drive and off-state limits, before release. [F, design note §2]
6. **U1 RF keep-out:** design note §4.2 counts ~13 mm metal-free antenna edge where the module recommends **18 mm minimum** at 4 mm depth. This is a recommended RF geometry, not a specified electrical absolute maximum; either restore the recommended exclusion geometry or explicitly accept the reduced-range experiment *before* ordering. [I §4.3]

## Method and references

Parsed the **checked-in** `hardware/board/elicio-v4.kicad_sch`, not just `v4_parts.py`: decoded embedded `lib_symbols` pin coordinates, top-level symbol positions, coincident global-label positions and `(no_connect ...)` flags. The schematic is a flat sheet with a label directly on every connected pin; all inspected pins were labelled or explicitly NC. Component values below are schematic properties; footprint and MPN are schematic properties where present. **Voltage ratings, ESR and dielectric are not encoded in the schematic** (and many passives have no MPN there). `v4_parts.py` supplies some intended MPNs, but this does not prove the assembled part or its DC-biased effective capacitance. “Close” below is the **maker's layout requirement**, not a placement verified from schematic coordinates. Design-note board-placement claims are labelled as such, not independently proven in this circuit check. Reference key (all PDF datasheets read **2026-09-25**):

## Contact safety chain: schematic connectivity, not physical creepage

The following are **complete endpoint lists** for each Contact net as parsed from global labels; each has exactly two pins, so no schematic branch can bypass its resistor. The AFE-side nodes also connect to the separate protected bench branches (and RLD feedback on `RLD_FB`), **after** their respective resistors:

| Electrode path | Contact-side net endpoints | AFE-side net endpoints relevant to contact | Severity | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 SIG1 → U2.4 | `SIG1`: P1.1, R1.1 only | `AFE_IN1P`: R1.2, R31.1, U2.4 | **note** | Keep R1 **220 kΩ** in series; inspect PCB continuity and physical solder bridges. |
| P2 SIG2 → U2.3 | `SIG2`: P2.1, R2.1 only | `AFE_IN1N`: R2.2, R32.1, U2.3 | **note** | Keep R2 **220 kΩ** in series; inspect PCB. |
| P3 REF → U2.29/30 | `REF`: P3.1, R3.1 only | `RLD_FB`: R3.2, R33.1, U2.29/30, R4.1, C1.1 | **note** | Keep R3 **220 kΩ** in series; inspect PCB. |
| J3.1 BENCH_SIG1 → U2.4 | `BENCH_SIG1`: J3.1, R31.2 only | `AFE_IN1P`: R31.1, R1.2, U2.4 | **note** | Keep R31 **220 kΩ** on removable tab; inspect as assembled. |
| J3.2 BENCH_SIG2 → U2.3 | `BENCH_SIG2`: J3.2, R32.2 only | `AFE_IN1N`: R32.1, R2.2, U2.3 | **note** | Keep R32 **220 kΩ** on tab; inspect. |
| J3.3 BENCH_REF → U2.29/30 | `BENCH_REF`: J3.3, R33.2 only | `RLD_FB`: R33.1, R3.2, U2.29/30 | **note** | Keep R33 **220 kΩ** on tab; inspect. |

Thus each path from one exposed electrode/header pin to its AFE node has **exactly one** 220 kΩ in the schematic. R1–R3 and R31–R33 are **parallel branches only on the protected AFE side**, not two resistors in series on one electrode. This does **not** establish PCB routing, tab cutting, patient leakage under faults, resistor working-voltage rating, contamination clearance, or IEC medical compliance; check the fabricated contact boundary before body use.
