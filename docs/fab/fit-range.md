# Fit limits

The selected shell is a reference CAD model, not a measured fit to Rolf's ear. No personal ear dimensions are tracked. No on-ear test exists.

The default curved arc is 48.4 mm. At the reference 3 mm bow, the straight chord is about 47.90 mm. The generator's `M1_gate` requires the straight attachment distance M1 to exceed the chord by 3 mm. This is a geometric placement allowance, not a validated tissue-pressure or comfort threshold.

Reference M1 defaults to 52 mm. It is an example, not anatomy data. The nominal 18 mm width and 8.1 mm body thickness also do not establish fit. Hook shape, preload, contact placement, lobe clearance, glasses and retention remain separate questions.

Population summaries cannot establish that the printed shell fits a specific ear. Earlier modeled population-fit percentages have been removed. Inspect a passive print and resolve fit and contact placement before considering powered wear. The snap must also be cycled and checked for access, fracture and retention.

Keep any later measurements and fit records under ignored `measurements/`. Keep output under `.reports/cad/`, never in the tracked reference export folders during routine generation. See [CAD instructions](../../scripts/cad/README.md) and [open gates](open-questions.md).
