# Fit range study — right-ear behind-the-ear shell

**Rolf: the current 47.90 mm body passes its length rule for about 63% of Korean adult women and men, but only about 10% of women and 45% of men in a second, mixed-age north-Indian sample. Those are *predicted geometric gate passes*, not demonstrated comfortable fits or a global population share. A genuinely small-ear design would need roughly a 27.5 mm chord and a new board/contact architecture; retaining a 30 mAh cell might mean ~12 mm thickness instead of 8.1 mm and ~4.3 instead of ~5.7 estimated hours. No existing board or printable shell is proven to meet that target.**

Study date: 2026-09-25. Public literature only. No board, shell, plan or firmware changed. This is a design envelope, not an order recommendation or a promise of universal fit. The term *M1* here means the **straight** distance from otobasion superius (upper ear-to-head attachment) to otobasion inferius (lower attachment), not the outermost ear height or the along-crease arc. Anatomical lower landmark depends on lobe attachment. All quoted percentages below assume a normal distribution *within each published sample*; none is a measured fit rate. None of the sources publishes an M1 percentile or raw M1 histogram; calculated tails are not published percentiles.

Sources (all pages and full-text tables read **2026-09-25**): [K] *Anthropometry of the External Ear in Korean Adults: A Multicenter Study* (2025), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12148551/ , DOI https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2025.40.e107 ; [I] *3D assessment of ear morphology* (2023), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10432210/ , DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobcr.2023.08.001 ; [P] *Morphological And Morphometric analysis of Human External Ear with Its Implications in Sex and Stature Estimation* (2025), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12032520/ , DOI https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.160629.3 ; Han Chinese original cited in [K]: Wang et al., *Computed tomography measurement of the auricle in Han population of north China* (2011), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bjps.2010.03.053 (comparison reproduced in [K], not an independent M1 series). These studies do not sample all continents, ear deformities, glasses, mastoid contour or actual earpiece wear. Age matters: [K] finds M1 rising with age (right-ear 20–29 means are ~3.8–3.9 mm below sex-wide means). A 99th-percentile *large* ear does not constrain the maximum chord under this **one-sided** length rule; other hook and contact dimensions may still constrain it.

The printed/normal-model 1st percentiles (mean − 2.326 SD) for **right** insertion/attachment length: Korean men 30.94, women 34.39 mm; north-Indian mixed-age men 37.97, women 31.88 mm. These low extremes are extrapolations (and Korean CT SDs are large); the lowest across the four rows is Korean men, **not** necessarily women. Neither the 1st nor 99th percentiles are published. The 99th upper ends would be respectively 77.46, 73.01, 62.53, 56.30 mm; these do not establish hook compatibility. Without adult-only sex strata for [I] or replicated direct M1 distributions across populations, a defensible worldwide 99% claim is impossible.
