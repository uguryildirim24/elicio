## What the public board schedule actually buys

[JLC standard PCBA schedule](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price) and [FPC extra charges](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/fpc-extra-charges), read 2026-09-25, apply to this two-sided 2-layer flex with six FR4 stiffeners; [assembly capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities) says flex needs dedicated fixtures *typically*, not that this design is exempt. The schedule lists **two flex fixtures $49.25 at 1–29 pieces**, pre-reflow **$0.016 per joint**, Standard feeder loading **$1.53 per Basic/Extended type**, and X-ray **$1.64 per inspected item for 1–10**. JLC's X-ray text says inspection is *required* for invisible joints such as BGA/QFN; U3 is a DSBGA and U1 is leadless. Therefore do **not** simply delete the fixture $49.25, pre-reflow $27.28 (1,705 pad-based estimated joints), feeder $41.31 (2026-09-25: 1 Basic + 23 Extended + 3 consigned types × $1.53, consigned applicability open), or X-ray $8.20 (U1-only *underestimate*: U3 and other leadless parts may add cost). Exact fixture use, which joint counts are charged, inspection scope, stiffener surcharge and whether J3 is manually placed need the order review; $0 savings counted here. The FPC page explicitly charges an extra stiffener fee for ≥4 per board; six remain. A public fixed fab quote for this released geometry cannot be obtained without uploading files, which this task forbids.

**Two versus five:** even supposing a two-piece Standard assembly is accepted, shared setup/stencil/fixtures/forward shipping remain. Linear illustrations from the [schedule](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price) at 341 SMT pads/board: pre-reflow **2×341×.016=$10.91 vs 5×341×.016=$27.28** (difference $16.37); placement **2×341×.0016=$1.09 vs $2.73** (difference $1.64); X-ray *U1 only* **2×$1.64=$3.28 vs 5×$1.64=$8.20** (difference $4.92). Direct JLC unit-tier part basket savings (five-board $48.211 baseline, 2026-09-25) and fabrication, handling, attrition and shipping differences are unknown. The sum **$22.93** of those *three* illustrative variable differences is not an order saving: lower quantities can change fees, minimums and manufacturing yield, and the five-board flex assembly minimum asserted in [orders-v2](orders-v2.md) was not independently proved by an accepted public checkout for this board. Don't book a two-board price; five remains the cost basis. Two boards mean fewer spares if a unit fails.

## Shipment map, and what the first build actually needs

At most the following consolidation should be pursued, not presumed: **(1)** PCB and JLC-library parts leave JLC together; direct-export U1/U3/J2 parts go **to JLC**, not Massachusetts, if one supplier can supply all three (unverified). **(2)** JLC3DP body + one lid: ask whether they can ride with (1); separate JLC3DP and JLCPCB checkouts do not prove one customs entry or one freight charge. **(3)** Protected fitted cell: potentially regulated *separate* shipment, not folded into an ordinary parts package without carrier acceptance. **(4)** Titanium screws, key, insulated charge cable, foam, meter if needed, bench leads/gel and debug accessories may consolidate at one US seller **only where that seller actually stocks the exact qualified articles**. **(5)** Spacer Express currently requires its own EU→MA parcel. Baseline likely parcel allowances (unchanged 2026-09-25) are $35 board, $40 parts→JLC, $22 shell, $25 cell, $40 EU standoff, $40 other US parts and $20 two-seller debug kit: **$222** across those lines; carrier/duty/tax allowances are additional. Combining all into one shipment is not supported. No freight/brokerage discount is booked on hope. [JLC shipping page](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/shipping-methods) gives no Massachusetts rate; read 2026-09-25.

**Needed for a safe one-earpiece build:** accepted five-board assembly (or a genuinely priced alternative), one fitted protected cell, insulated charging interface, three qualified skin-contact screws and standoffs, foam, one printed body and **one** lid, off-body meter/polarity and continuity checks (buy meter if Rolf lacks one), first firmware load *somewhere*, and tests/bench leads and gel for S2 if validation proceeds. Do not remove the electrode bench kit merely because it is not worn at S4. A nickel screen is either performed or explicitly waived by Rolf in writing per [plan-v2 §10](plan-v2.md); omission without the waiver is not a cut. Extra lid and black finish are the only no-function print options here (undyed finish still needs skin-contact evidence/acceptance). Existing $25 likely charge-cable allowance has no qualified exact SKU; it cannot be zeroed.

**First-load probe:** the least expensive specifically priced candidate in the worksheet is the [Adafruit Raspberry Pi Debug Probe #5699](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5699), **one at $12**, advertised in stock, CMSIS-DAP SWD **nominal 3.3 V I/O**. Mate it through one [Tag-Connect TC2030-IDC-NL](https://www.tag-connect.com/product/tc2030-idc-nl), **$33.95**, plus jumper wiring, *only after* [board-v4 §7](board-v4-design.md) / plan G4 checks J4 pad map, target's 3.0 V U5 rail under load, both directions' absolute I/O limits, ground-first sequence and the factory image. Nominal 3.3 V is **not** proof it is safe at 3.0 V; no lower-priced bare Pico/pogo-pins substitution is qualified or compatible with R2. If G4 fails, retain the full kit allowance and stop; do not wire a 3.3 V supply into J4 target VDD. Factory programming saves the kit only if the assembler actually supplies, flashes and verifies the **correct** bootloader/image as part of a priced service (no public acceptance shown).
