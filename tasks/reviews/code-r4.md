## Needs a decision

24. **CABLE_EXIT at s 36 cuts a board pad.** Plan §3.3 puts the Ø2.0 exit at s 36, y 3 in the posterior wall. Plan §3.3 BOARD_ZONE puts the inferior posterior pad at s 36.4–37.9, y 1.5–4.3. The exit removes 0.99 mm³ of that pad in packings A and C; B's longer board zone moves the pad away. The provisional file uses s 35.0 (0.4 clear) and says why. Choices: plan erratum to s 35, a smaller exit, or pads inset from the exit. Closes: coordinator (plan erratum), or Rolf.
25. **E1 over the reference pocket.** If the closure test passes and order 2 keeps E1 (plan §3.6), the lid's web (s 45.6–46.4, y LID_Y − 0.8 to LID_Y) and tongue root sit 2.48 mm³ inside the Ø7.5 reference pocket (plan §3.3: centre s 43, so it reaches s 46.75). The body's web pocket and tongue slot also break the wall around it (8.3 mm³ of the 1.0 band missing). Q21's lug already reaches y 8.23. Today `CLOSURE_PASSED = true` fails `KEEPOUT_REF_air`. Choices: move CONTACT_REF about 1 mm toward −s (interface change, and Q17 may move it anyway), move the web and tongue, or order 2 without E1 (interface item 6). Closes: Rolf, with Q21 and Q17.
26. **WP7a coordinates need a placement re-run before WP8.** The pads, tab angles and reference route were searched for the plan §3.3 sites. The shell now refuses other sites (`PLACEMENT_contacts`). The brief's reading, "WP8 proper is a parameter set and a run", therefore needs WP6's `search_tab_degrees` re-run on WP7a's sites first. Sequencing for the coordinator.
27. **A thin Stage B body cannot hold the cell.** At VARIANT thin, LID_Y is 6.0. The cell plus foam top is 7.2 on the 1.5 floor (−1.2 mm pre-CAD; 178 mm³ into the lid measured). Plan §10 "Open for Rolf" item 5 lets Rolf pick thickness after 3.7. If he picks thin, order 2 needs a different cell or pocket. The Stage B default builds full only. Closes: Rolf's 3.7 answer, with Q18.
