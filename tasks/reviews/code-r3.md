## Needs a decision

20. **Packing: C or something else.** Only C closes (BODY_WIDTH 17 → 20, board 19 × 15.5). It needs the lead pads moved from interface §4 to SIG1 (7.5, 29.35), SIG2 (13.5, 21.35), REF (5.5, 29.35), and the reference wire crossing the SIG1 lead once on the floor near (3.9, 33.7) where nothing is above it. The plan does not forbid a crossing; that is my reading. Without it C needs a 2 mm lead bend instead of the plan's 3. If Rolf does not want C: a shorter solder lug (outside plan §4's crimp text) or moving the contacts. `docs/fab/packing-options.md` has the one-line answer. Q15 closes with it.
21. **Reference lug in the tail pocket.** TE 31428 upright is 1.96 thick against the plan's 1.5 envelope, and bent at the ring edge it reaches about y 8.2 against the lid at 8.0; an outward barrel passes the Ø7.5 pocket wall. The wire then leaves the barrel pointing at the lid and has to turn back down to the channel at y 2.5–4.1 inside a 7.5 mm pocket, which a 3 mm bend radius does not allow. Choices: accept a tighter bend there and check it as installed (plan §10 already asks for that check), bend the tab at a smaller radius toward the axis over the Kapton, or change the reference termination. Plan §3.3 KEEPOUT_REF is Rolf's to change.
22. **Signal lug barrel height.** TE 31428's barrel is 1.96 across; plan §3.3 KEEPOUT_SIGNAL gives the flat tab 1.5 of height. On the floor the barrel tops out at y 3.46, under the board at 4.3. The placement already keeps parts and copper off the barrel strip, so C does not change; the plan's envelope height does. Plan erratum to 2.0 for WP8's keep-out solids, or Rolf keeps 1.5 and WP5 finds a flatter barrel.
23. **28 AWG in a 26–22 AWG barrel.** Carried from r1: plan §4 asks for a 26–28 AWG barrel; 31428 is rated 26–22. The assembly sheet folds the strip and solders the crimp. Rolf accepts that, or WP5 finds a 28 AWG barrel of similar length.

Q6 (nut metal) and Q7 (screw SKU and dome) stay with Rolf as before; WP5b's §8.2 and §8.3 give him the table without a recommendation.
